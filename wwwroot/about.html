Community map of Armenia

The Community map is a website for map-based access to spatial data from the Armenian open data community. It is an initiative of Open Data Armenia.

The Community map is a fully open architecture. When you access data through it, you are typically accessing the data directly from our data catalog or from the services of the data publishers.

The Community map:

  • provides easy access to authoritative and other spatial data to government, business and the public
  • facilitates the opening of data by national and local government bodies
  • provides an open framework of geospatial data services that supports commercial and community innovation

To see what data is available, open the map and click Explore data, then expand the group names.

How to use the Community map

Go to maps.opendata.am. Most operations are obvious; the Help button in the map shows a guided tour and instructions.

For further information email opendata@opendata.am. Feel free to send us feedback and bug reports. The source code is on GitHub.

Contributing data to the Community map

Please write to opendata@opendata.am if you would like to add your data to the Community map.
